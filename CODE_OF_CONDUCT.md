# Code Of Conduct

This project should be a practical, respectful place to improve local-first TRMNL agent status tooling.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, constructive, and specific.
- Assume contributors care about privacy and safety.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Help make examples safer, clearer, and easier to reuse.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sharing another person’s private information or credentials.
- Submitting real secrets, private payloads, screenshots, logs, emails, calendar content, or customer data.
- Repeatedly pushing unsafe patterns after maintainers explain the concern.

## Reporting

For security issues, follow `SECURITY.md`.

For conduct issues, open a private maintainer contact path once the public repository publishes one. Until then, do not post sensitive details in public issues.

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove issues, pull requests, comments, or examples that expose secrets or private data.
